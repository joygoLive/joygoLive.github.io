UQH

Beta
Universal Quantum Hub · Quantum computing

Vendor-neutral middleware for quantum computing. Write in the SDK you already use, and reach every connected processor through one pipeline: analysis, optimization, noise simulation, error mitigation, QEC, and cost estimation before you submit.

  • Nine SDK frameworks, eight hardware vendors, one interface
  • Rank devices on evidence before spending QPU time
  • UI, REST API, CLI, and an MCP server for AI agents

AMC

In development
AI Motion Coaching · Community sports

Real-time exercise-form coaching from a single phone camera. A trainer's judgment criteria encoded as joint-angle rules, normalized against your own body baseline. The feedback names what to fix instead of scoring a similarity you cannot read.

  • Runs on the device. No video leaves the phone
  • One spoken cue during the set, a full report after it
  • Reps it could not judge are named, never quietly counted

BOR

Testnet
Blockchain On-chain Raffle · Base

An on-chain raffle where 90% of ticket sales become the prize. The 10% fee is a constant compiled into the contract, and the drand round each future draw will use is already determined. The draw can be checked rather than trusted.

  • National lotteries return about 50%. BOR returns 90%
  • No setter for the fee, no proxy to swap the logic
  • BLS12-381 beacon verification on-chain, against RFC 9380 vectors

WCP

Beta
Web Capability Probe · Measurement

Checks that run in your own browser, reporting what the device actually exposes to the web. Every item is invoked rather than looked up, because a browser answering "yes" and a browser doing the thing are two different answers.

  • Execution, not a support table. Five states, not yes/no
  • Separates device limits from browser policy and our own headers
  • Send a measurement and get your device's tier and full breakdown back

Bring an idea

joygoLive builds and runs it
You plan and sell it
Whether it works, we find out together

The scarce half of a product is rarely the code. If you have something worth building and no one to build it, that half is covered.

What I bring Feasibility first — whether it can be done at all, and how. Then design, build, deploy, operate, repair. Four unrelated fields above were built this way; the domain is not the constraint.
What you bring What it should be and who it is for. The wording, the channel, the people who need to hear it. No finished plan required — a clear problem is enough.
Build it together Bring it as it is — an idea you find interesting, or a problem you want solved. We build a small version first and find out together. If it does not look like it can work, you hear that early rather than late.

An idea you post is readable only by you and joygoLive. The list keeps the start of the title, the date, and the status, so the record that it was already here stays without giving the idea away. Posting needs no account and no contact details; you set a password instead, and replies come back as comments on your own post. Ideas are not deleted once posted.
