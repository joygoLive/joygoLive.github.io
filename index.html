What has shipped so far

Quantum infrastructure, exercise coaching, an on-chain raffle, web measurement — and two still being built: a memory-training room and a wireless environment check. Fields that share nothing with each other, already running rather than described. Each was built here and is operated here: not licensed, not resold.

UQH

Beta
Universal Quantum Hub · Quantum computing

Vendor-neutral middleware for quantum computing. Write in the SDK you already use, and reach every connected processor through one pipeline: analysis, optimization, noise simulation, error mitigation, QEC, and cost estimation before you submit.

  • Nine SDK frameworks, eight hardware vendors, one interface
  • Rank devices on evidence before spending QPU time
  • UI, REST API, CLI, and an MCP server for AI agents

AMC

In development
AI Motion Coaching · Community sports

Real-time exercise-form coaching from a single phone camera. A trainer's judgment criteria encoded as joint-angle rules, normalized against your own body baseline. The feedback names what to fix instead of scoring a similarity you cannot read.

  • Runs on the device. No video leaves the phone
  • One spoken cue during the set, a full report after it
  • Reps it could not judge are named, never quietly counted
Read more

BOR

Testnet
Blockchain On-chain Raffle · Base

An on-chain raffle where 90% of ticket sales become the prize. The 10% fee is a constant compiled into the contract, and the drand round each future draw will use is already determined. The draw can be checked rather than trusted.

  • National lotteries return about 50%. BOR returns 90%
  • No setter for the fee, no proxy to swap the logic
  • BLS12-381 beacon verification on-chain, against RFC 9380 vectors

WCP

Beta
Web Capability Probe · Measurement

Checks that run in your own browser, reporting what the device actually exposes to the web. Every item is invoked rather than looked up, because a browser answering "yes" and a browser doing the thing are two different answers.

  • Execution, not a support table. Five states, not yes/no
  • Separates device limits from browser policy and our own headers
  • Send a measurement and get your device's tier and full breakdown back
Read more

LOCI

In development
Method of Loci · 3D memory training

A room you look around and place things in, then recall where they were. The 3D is not decoration: the method of loci is spatial to begin with. Built as a testbed for the whole pipeline — authoring, handoff, serving, and measuring what a device does with it.

  • A room is one JSON file. Planning edits it; development never touches code for a new room
  • Four interchangeable runtimes draw the same scene — WebGL2, three.js, WebGPU, Canvas 2D
  • Every session reports frames, jank, input lag and load time from the device itself
Read more

BCON

In development
Broadcast Commute Observation Networks · Wireless environment

Checks the wireless environment in range and names what is wrong with it — no encryption, one name answering from several radios, protections never switched on — then says what to change for each. Scored per place, with the parts always shown.

  • Receives only. No connecting, no authenticating, no probing
  • One risk score per place, always shown with its parts
  • Every finding carries the setting or habit that removes it

Whether it works, we find out together

The scarce half of a product is rarely the code. If you have something worth building and no one to build it, joygoLive covers that half: design, implementation, deployment, and running it for as long as it runs on its own. You carry the planning, the positioning, and getting it in front of people.

What I bring Working out whether it can be done at all, and how. Then design, implementation, deployment, and everything after: operating it, keeping it up, improving it. The services above were built that way in fields that share nothing with each other, so the domain is not the constraint.
What you bring What it should be and who it is for; the wording, the channel, the people who need to hear about it. Nothing here needs to be a finished plan. A clear problem is enough to start.
Build it together Bring it as it is: an idea you find interesting, or a problem you want solved. It does not have to be a business plan. We build a small version first and find out together whether it holds up; if it does not look like it can work, you hear that early rather than late.

An idea you post is readable only by you and joygoLive. The list keeps the start of the title, the date, and the status, so the record that it was already here stays without giving the idea away. Posting needs no account and no contact details; you set a password instead, and replies come back as comments on your own post. Ideas are not deleted once posted.
