What has shipped so far

Quantum infrastructure, exercise coaching, an on-chain raffle, web measurement, a wireless environment check, shared listening with an AI DJ — and one still being built: a memory-training room. Unrelated fields, all in production. Each was built and is operated in-house: not licensed, not resold.

UQH

Beta
Universal Quantum Hub · Quantum computing

Vendor-neutral middleware for quantum computing. Write in the SDK you already use, and reach every connected processor through one pipeline: analysis, optimization, noise simulation, error mitigation, QEC, and cost estimation before you submit.

  • Nine SDK frameworks, eight hardware vendors, one interface
  • Rank devices on evidence before spending QPU time
  • UI, REST API, CLI, and an MCP server for AI agents

AMC

Beta
AI Motion Coaching · Community sports

Real-time exercise-form coaching from a single phone camera. A trainer's judgment criteria encoded as joint-angle rules, normalized against your own body baseline. Feedback names the correction instead of reporting an uninterpretable similarity score.

  • On-device inference. No video leaves the phone
  • One spoken cue during the set, a full report after it
  • Reps that could not be judged are reported with the reason and excluded from the count
Read more

BOR

Testnet
Blockchain On-chain Raffle · Base

An on-chain raffle where 90% of ticket sales become the prize. The 10% fee is a constant compiled into the contract, and the drand round each future draw will use is already determined. Anyone can verify the draw on-chain.

  • National lotteries return about 50%. BOR returns 90%
  • No setter for the fee, no proxy to swap the logic
  • BLS12-381 beacon verification on-chain, against RFC 9380 vectors

WCP

Beta
Web Capability Probe · Measurement

Checks that run in your own browser, reporting what the device actually exposes to the web. Every item is invoked rather than looked up, because a browser answering "yes" and a browser doing the thing are two different answers.

  • Execution, not a support table. Five states, not yes/no
  • Separates device limits from browser policy and server response headers
  • Send a measurement and get your device's tier and full breakdown back
Read more

LOCI

In development
Method of Loci · 3D memory training

A room you look around and place things in, then recall where they were. The 3D is not decoration: the method of loci is a spatial technique. Built as a testbed for the full pipeline: authoring, handoff, serving, and on-device measurement.

  • A room is one JSON file. Planning edits it; adding a room requires no code change
  • Five interchangeable runtimes draw the same scene — WebGL2, three.js, WebGPU, Canvas 2D, Unity WebGL
  • Every session reports frames, jank, input lag and load time from the device itself
Read more

BCON

Beta
Broadcast Commute Observation Networks · Wireless environment

Identifies weaknesses in the wireless environment in range — no encryption, one SSID answering from several APs, protections left off — and lists the corrective setting for each. Scored per place, with the components always shown.

  • Receives only. No connecting, no authenticating, no probing
  • One risk score per place, always shown with its parts
  • Each finding carries its remediation: a setting change or a connection habit
Read more

MP

Beta
Music Play · Music everyone hears together

One server clock, one schedule: everyone connected hears the same song at the same point. Requests with stories play in the next slots and an AI DJ reads them over the intro. A hand-curated catalogue keeps the music going in between.

  • No audio served: the server holds the clock and schedule, each browser opens its own embed
  • First come, first served plus weights: a story outranks a bare request, waiting time never stops accruing
  • DJ script from the catalogue only, two voices, off on your device if you want music alone

The missing half of a product is rarely the code. If you have something worth building and no one to build it, joygoLive covers that half: design, implementation, deployment, and operation until it runs on its own. Planning, positioning, and distribution stay with you.

What joygoLive brings Technical feasibility and approach first. Then design, implementation, deployment, operation, and iteration. The services above were built this way across unrelated fields; the domain is not the constraint.
What you bring Product definition and target users; messaging, channel, and reaching the people who need it. A finished plan is not required. A clearly stated problem is enough.
A small build, verified together Bring it as it is: an idea, or a problem you want solved. A business plan is not required. We build a minimal version first and verify together whether it holds; if it does not look viable, you hear that early.

A posted idea is readable only by you and joygoLive. The list shows the start of the title, the date, and the status: the submission is on record without the idea being disclosed.
