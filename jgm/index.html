JGM · Service marketplace

An escrow marketplace for renovation work.

Customers post one request and receive quotes from verified renovation pros whose categories and regions match. The chosen quote becomes an e-contract, the payment is held in escrow, and the money is released stage by stage as the customer approves each milestone. Pros are paid out in settlement runs, with the commission invoiced automatically.

Open JGM How a project flows What this does not claim

One project, three sides. The customer, the pro and the operator work on the same project: quote, contract, escrow payment, milestone approvals, payout and the ledger entries behind each step. A role switch moves between the three views in one browser, and the whole path is replayed as automated checks after every change.

Design

Three decisions that shaped it

Most marketplace briefs differ by industry and share the same loop. The loop is the product; the industry is a setting.

The industry is a configurationCategories, regions, the request form, quote inclusions, the milestone split, review products and trust copy come from one industry definition. Renovation is the first; matching, escrow, settlement, chat and the operator console stay unchanged when another industry is added.
Money moves through a double-entry ledgerPayment received, funds held, a milestone released with the commission split out, payout, refund and review credit are each a balanced journal entry. Five reconciliation checks compare the ledger with payments, projects, payouts and review orders.
Integration points in place of providersPayment, payout, identity, business status, account holder, messaging, e-tax invoice, e-contract, report drafting and file storage sit behind ten interfaces. The contract includes idempotency keys for every write and a query when the result of a call is unknown.
Projects

From request to final payment

The customer side, in the order a project goes through it.

RequestCategory, region, property type and size, occupancy, start date, budget range, details and site photos. The request reaches every approved pro whose categories and regions match, and stays open for 14 days.
Quote comparisonPrice, duration, warranty, rating, completed projects and each inclusion side by side, with the milestone split of every quote. Pros see how many quotes a request has, never the amounts of the others.
E-contract and escrow paymentThe accepted quote fixes the contract terms and both sides sign. Payment runs through a checkout window by card, virtual account or easy pay; the server approves only the amount on its own ledger, whatever the browser returns.
Release per milestoneThe pro requests approval for each stage in order, and the customer approves after inspection. A request left unanswered for seven days is approved automatically. Before the first approval the customer can cancel for a full refund.

Pros

The pro side

From verification to the payout of a single milestone.

Onboarding and verificationPhone identity check at sign-up, business registration number validated by its checksum and checked for active status, operator approval, and a payout account accepted only when the holder matches the owner or business name.
Leads and quotesOpen requests in the pro's categories and regions. A quote carries the amount, duration, start date, inclusions, a message and the milestone percentages, with the commission and the net payout shown before sending.
Settlement and payoutApproved milestones minus commission become the payable balance. A settlement run requests payouts from the payout service; on completion the commission for that period is issued as an e-tax invoice to the pro's business.
Chat without contact detailsOne thread per customer and pro. Quotes, acceptance, payment, milestone requests and approvals are posted into the same thread as system messages, linked to the project.
Review

Paid expert review that feeds the marketplace

A fixed-price product next to the quote-based one: a quote check at ₩49,000 and a scope and budget plan at ₩99,000.

Order and materialsPayment first, then the description and files: quotes, photos, floor plans. Cancelling before the materials are submitted refunds the full amount.
A structured reportWork items with scope, difficulty and a fair price range, a budget and duration range, risks and recommendations. The reviewer starts from a generated draft behind the report-drafting interface and edits it before delivery. One revision is included.
Converted into a requestThe report fills a new request form: category, budget, scope and the points to confirm. Converting confirms the review, and its revenue is recognized.
Fee credited on contractWhen the converted request becomes a contract, the review fee is deducted from the payment. The platform carries the credit as an expense entry, and cancelling the project restores it.
Operations

The operator console

Approvals, money and integrations on one console, laid out for phones as well.

Work queuesPros awaiting approval, reviews to write, virtual accounts awaiting deposit and pros with a payable balance. Thirty-day GMV, commission, match rate and the median time to a first quote.
MoneyPayments with a query to the gateway, settlement runs, payouts, the trial balance, reconciliation checks and the journal of every project.
Payment gateway consoleThe simulated gateway exposes what a real one does to the platform: deposits to virtual accounts, expirations, webhook resends, and fault injection for a declined payment, duplicate webhooks and delayed webhooks.
Rules as settingsCommission in basis points and its rounding, auto-approval and auto-confirmation days, payout delay, minimum payout, request lifetime, quotes per request and the review credit, changed without a deployment.

Limits

What this does not claim

The marketplace loop is implemented end to end. Money, identity, the businesses and the people are outside the scope.

No real payment or payoutThe gateway and the payout service are simulated. No money moves, no card details are collected and no bank account is contacted.
No real verificationIdentity, business status and account holder checks are simulated. Only the business number checksum is a real calculation.
Fictional pros, customers and ratingsSeventy days of history are generated through the platform's own flows, and seeded accounts keep adding requests, quotes and ratings. The demo accounts are shared by every visitor and can be reset from the console.
First phase, one industryRenovation with quote-based matching and commission only. Dispute mediation with partial refunds, detection of contact details shared in chat, and booking-type matching are not implemented.
